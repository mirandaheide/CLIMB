Let's get this thing going!

Setup Your Card

Pick your climbing style (bouldering or rope climbing) and your grading convention. "Grading system" is the convention your gym uses to label their routes and boulders.

Select the colors and other features available at your gym. Once ready, hit "Generate Card" and you're ready to climb!

At the Gym

Each square is a challenge to complete. Send a climb that matches the description, then tap the square to mark it complete. Get five in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win!

Pro Tips

Circuit grading? Select "Yes" if your gym uses color-based difficulty levels. In this case, be sure to only select colors that correspond to your target levels of difficulty.

Free Climb square? That's your freebie - it's already marked!

Wildcards? "Climber's Choice" and "Friend's Choice" let you pick any climb.

Stuck? Generate a new card anytime - no two cards are the same!
